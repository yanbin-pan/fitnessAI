Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026. This page covers the Zabaione waitlist only.

Who we are

Zabaione is an AI food and fitness coach, made by an independent founder in the United Kingdom. For anything about your data, email hello@zabaione.app.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • If you choose to tell us, how you heard about Zabaione.
  • Where the visit came from: campaign tags in the link, the website that sent you (its name only), your country and your browser language.

We don't use cookies or tracking pixels on this page.

Why

To email you when Zabaione is ready for you, with occasional launch news, and to learn which channels bring people in. We rely on your consent, which you give by joining. We never sell or share your email.

Where it's kept and for how long

Sign-ups are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. We keep them until Zabaione launches publicly, or until you ask us to remove them, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, or leave the list, by emailing hello@zabaione.app. Every email we send has a way to unsubscribe. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
